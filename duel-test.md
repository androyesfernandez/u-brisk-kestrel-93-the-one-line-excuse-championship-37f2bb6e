# Duel-test record — The One-Line Excuse Championship

## Method

No OpenRouter key is available on this VM, so the duel used proxy judging
under the kit brief verbatim — not the house z-ai/glm-5.3-flash judge.
Two rival drafters produced 20 drafts; a rival critic then attacked them
and 14 were killed outright. The 5 survivors went to a round-robin final,
each pair judged in both orders, scored against the 0.60 bar.

## Critic fixes applied to the winner

- The first clause was cut down, taking the line from 124 characters to
  95, so the whole excuse lands in one clean sentence.
- An em-dash was used for the snap before the punchline ("— it had a
  newer timestamp."), tightening the turn.
- An archive check found no collision on the "newer timestamp" angle —
  no earlier entry rides the same joke.
